## Descriptions

**One line (up to 60 characters):**

**Short (up to 160 characters):**

**Long (about 120 words):**
Contractor Leads is lead-finding software for marketing agencies and sales teams that sell to
home-service contractors. Pick a trade and a city or state, and it pulls every matching business from live
Google Places data, cross-checked with Yelp and the business website. Each lead gets the owner's name,
verified contact details, Meta ad status and an AI score for its marketing gaps (website, SEO, ads), ranked
Hot, Warm or Nurture. Outreach Studio writes a cold email, SMS and call script from each lead's own audit,
and a built-in pipeline tracks deals. It covers 18 trades, from roofing and HVAC to pool services, in the
US, Canada, the UK, Australia and New Zealand. Start with 10 free leads; paid plans start at $19.99 a month.
It does not sell homeowner leads to contractors.

**Key features (bullets):**
- Lead Finder: every business in a trade and market from live Google Places data
- Owner enrichment: owner names and verified emails; unverified fields are left blank
- AI lead scoring: website, SEO, ads and marketing gaps scored 0 to 100, Hot / Warm / Nurture
- Meta Ads Intel: whether each business is running Facebook or Instagram ads
- Outreach Studio: cold email, SMS and call scripts written from each lead's audit
- Pipeline CRM and Lead Map; CSV, Excel, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zapier, Make and webhook exports
